Understand rooms and portals

Connect an interior to the exterior and diagnose visibility problems without confusing portals with door holes.

Three separate parts of an entrance#

A door opening cuts the visible architectural shell. Collision determines whether the player can pass through that space. A portal connects room visibility. Creating or fixing one does not automatically prove the other two work.

For a two-room house, the intended visibility route is usually exterior → first room → second room. The app calls the exterior relationship Exterior / limbo. It is not another furnished room that you need to draw.

Create a connection#

  1. In Floor plan, make sure the rooms meet at the intended wall. Select Doorway and click that wall.
  2. Enable Create portal with new opening before placing the doorway, or select an existing opening and enable Visibility portal.
  3. Inspect the opening's location and size. The portal should describe the opening you actually intend to see through, rather than an unrelated point in the room.
  4. Open Rooms and portals. Set Inspect connectivity from to Exterior / limbo, then inspect each room in turn.

The connectivity graph and portal list should explain how the player sees into the house and between its rooms. Use them to find a missing connection before attempting another export.

Check objects and direction#

Under Interior entity assignments, a placed mesh can use its geometry position or be assigned to a specific room. Check an object's assignment if it appears to belong to the wrong room or disappears at a doorway.

The opening controls include Reverse portal direction. Inspect the portal's current relationship before changing it; reversing every portal is not a general repair for a visibility issue. Keep one change at a time so you can compare the result.

Troubleshoot in order#

  • A wall still fills the doorway: inspect the opening in the floor plan and generated shell.
  • The opening looks clear but blocks walking: inspect its collision solids.
  • The next room disappears: inspect its portal connection, direction and entity assignments.
  • A room is disconnected: trace a complete path from the exterior instead of only checking neighboring room names.

Rebuild after correcting the project. In FiveM, check visibility from both sides of every portal and walk through each doorway. Record the result against the exact resource ZIP you tested, as described in the in-game checklist.
